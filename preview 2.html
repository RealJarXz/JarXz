📚 How Peptide Reconstitution Works

Peptides usually come as a **freeze-dried powder** inside a vial. Before use, the powder must be mixed with **bacteriostatic water**.

The amount of water added determines the **concentration** of the peptide. This calculator converts your planned weekly dosage into **syringe units**.

The planner also estimates:

  • How much to inject each week
  • Remaining peptide in the vial
  • When the vial will run out
